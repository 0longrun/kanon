# Árnyékkánon — render-promptok a 11 kapu tereihez

Kapunként: KÉP-prompt (angolul, minden generátorral működik) + VIDEÓ-prompt.
A színkódok a játék tényleges palettájából jönnek — így a render illik ahhoz,
amit a játékos a kapun belül lát.

**Közös stílusblokk** — ezt fűzd MINDEN prompt elé (a borító festett stílusához igazítva):

> Dark painterly storybook illustration, matte oil-paint texture, muted antique
> gold accents, soft volumetric haze, atmospheric depth, no text, no watermark,
> no photorealism, no modern objects.

Képarány: állóban `9:16` (mobil), fekvőben `21:9` vagy `16:9` (desktop).
Videó: 6–10 mp, seamless loop, lassú kameramozgás — Runway/Sora/Veo kompatibilis.

---

## I. Javne — A vita háza (udvar, i. sz. ~90 · tekercs)
Paletta: éjbarna ég `#241c10` → borostyán horizont `#d9a45f`, agyagfal `#6e5c42`, arany akcens `#e0b060`. Sziluett: hegyek + városka.

**KÉP:** A sun-baked clay-brick courtyard in ancient Judea at amber dusk, low
rectangular walls with scroll niches, elderly sages in homespun robes debating
around unrolled Torah scrolls on stone benches, warm ochre light raking across
dust, distant hills and a small town silhouette on the horizon, embers of sunset
`#d9a45f` against a deep umber sky `#241c10`, golden accents `#e0b060`.

**VIDEÓ:** Slow dolly forward through the courtyard gate toward the circle of
debating sages, dust motes drifting in amber light, scroll edges lifting in a
faint breeze, oil-lamp flames flickering, shadows of gesturing hands moving on
the clay wall. 8s seamless loop, no cuts.

## II. Alexandria — A 39. levél (bazilika, i. sz. 367 · papirusz)
Paletta: mélykék ég `#0e1a2c`, kőkék oszlopok `#5a6d84`, halvány arany horizont `#e8c98a`, akcens `#d9c48a`. Sziluett: város + hegy.

**KÉP:** Interior of an early Christian basilica in Alexandria, tall stone
columns in cool blue-grey `#5a6d84`, a bishop at a lectern dictating a letter by
candlelight, TWO separate shelves of papyrus codices — one gilded and ordered,
one plain and overflowing — pale gold light `#e8c98a` falling through high
windows into deep blue shadow `#0e1a2c`.

**VIDEÓ:** Slow lateral tracking shot past the columns, candle flames trembling,
a scribe's quill moving, dust in the god-rays between the two bookshelves, the
camera settling on the plain shelf as a codex is quietly moved from one shelf
to the other. 8s loop.

## III. Nag Hammadi — A korsó (sivatagi éj, i. sz. ~370 · kódex)
Paletta: ibolyafekete ég `#070510` holddal és csillagokkal, lila derengés `#4a2a55`, homok `#4a382c`, levendula akcens `#c9a0e8`. Sziluett: dűnék + hegy.

**KÉP:** Night at the foot of a desert cliff in Upper Egypt, full moon and dense
stars over violet darkness `#4a2a55`, three monks in rough robes lowering
thirteen leather-bound codices into a large sealed clay jar in a sand pit,
lantern glow on their faces, lavender moonlight `#c9a0e8` on the dune ridges,
profound secrecy and tenderness.

**VIDEÓ:** Static wide shot slowly pushing in: the last codex is placed, the lid
settles, sand trickles from a monk's hand over the jar, lantern flame gutters
in the wind, stars glimmer. 10s, single take, loop on the drifting sand.

## IV. Váránaszí — A hang, amelyet senki sem írt (gát-lépcsők, i. sz. ~750 · pálmalevél)
Paletta: tealzöld ég `#12303a`, sáfrány napkelte `#ffb45f`, réz-arany akcens `#ffb347`, templomfal `#8a6a35`. Sziluett: templomok + pálmák. Nap a képen.

**KÉP:** Dawn over the Ganges at Varanasi, saffron sun `#ffb45f` rising through
teal mist `#2a5a58`, stone ghat steps down to glittering water, a teacher
chanting with closed eyes surrounded by seated students — NO books, only sound:
faint golden ripples in the air around his voice, temple spires and palms in
silhouette, lotus blossoms on the water.

**VIDEÓ:** Slow crane down from the temple spires to the water, sun flare
breathing through mist, ripples radiating from the chanting figure like sound
made visible, a palm frond swaying, water glittering `#ffb347`. 8s loop.

## V. Csienkang — A katalógus (tusfestmény, i. sz. ~515 · tus és selyem)
Paletta: SZÁNDÉKOSAN VILÁGOS — celadon köd `#9ab5a8`, csontfehér ég `#b8c9bd`, mélyzöld akcens `#2e4a3a`. Sziluett: pagodák + hegyek. Sumi-e stílus!

**KÉP:** Bright ink-wash (sumi-e) landscape: pale celadon river mist `#9ab5a8`,
soft grey pagoda silhouettes dissolving into white sky `#e8e2c8`, a scholar at
a low desk on a riverside veranda writing a catalogue on silk scrolls, bamboo
leaning into frame, a single deep-green ink stroke `#2e4a3a` as accent — mostly
empty space, luminous fog, LIGHT tonality (not dark).

**VIDEÓ:** Mist slowly drifting across the river, revealing then veiling the
pagodas, the scholar's brush lifting between strokes, a heron gliding through
the fog band. 10s, meditative, loop on the mist cycle.

## VI. Nippur — Az agyag emlékezete (ziggurat, i. e. ~1740 · agyagtábla)
Paletta: szilvalila ég `#241a33` → parázs-narancs horizont `#e8874a`, agyag `#59431f`, arany akcens `#f0c060`. Sziluett: ziggurat + város.

**KÉP:** An Old Babylonian scribal school (edubba) at dusk, mud-brick room open
to a plum-violet sky `#241a33` with an ember-orange horizon `#e8874a`, a great
ziggurat silhouette beyond the rooftops, clay tablets stacked on reed mats, a
teacher-scribe pressing a stylus into wet clay by oil-lamp light, golden lamp
glow `#f0c060` on cuneiform wedges.

**VIDEÓ:** Slow push-in over the scribe's shoulder onto the wet tablet, wedge
marks appearing stroke by stroke, lamp flame swaying, the ziggurat darkening as
the horizon ember fades. 8s loop.

## VII. Ktesziphón — A feltalált ábécé (tűzoltár, i. sz. ~350 · emlékezet és tinta)
Paletta: vörösbarna ég `#1a0c14` → izzó narancs horizont `#ff7a3a`, láng-akcens `#ff9040`, fal `#7a4a24`. Sziluett: hegyek + város (Taq Kasra ív).

**KÉP:** A Zoroastrian fire altar under an ember sky `#ff7a3a`, the great brick
arch of Ctesiphon in distant silhouette, magi in white robes tending an eternal
flame on a stone pedestal, sparks rising, one priest writing a newly invented
alphabet onto parchment — one letter for every shade of sound — firelight
`#ff9040` against deep maroon dusk `#4a1c22`.

**VIDEÓ:** Locked-off shot of the altar: flame breathing, sparks spiraling
upward, heat shimmer over the coals, the scribe's hand adding one letterform,
the arch dissolving into dusk haze. 8s seamless fire loop.

## VIII. Maní — A tűz (máglya, 1562. július 12. · kéregpapír)
Paletta: vérvörös éj `#0d0508`, égő horizont `#8a2a1a`, láng `#ff5a30`, föld `#321610`. Sziluett: dzsungel + hegyek.

**KÉP:** Night in the Yucatán: a great pyre of burning Maya bark-paper codices
and carved cult images, hieroglyphic pages curling in flames `#ff5a30`, Fransiscan
friars as dark silhouettes before the blaze, Maya onlookers' faces lit with
grief at the edge of darkness, jungle canopy silhouette against a blood-red sky
`#8a2a1a`, sparks ascending like lost constellations. Solemn, not sensational.

**VIDEÓ:** Slow pull-back from a single burning glyph page — the glyph's line
glowing before it blackens — revealing the whole pyre and the watching
silhouettes, embers rising into the dark. 10s, single take, ember loop.

## IX. Medina — A hét másolat (árkádívek, i. sz. ~653 · pergamen)
Paletta: hajnal előtti kék `#0a1626` → jégkék derengés `#7fb0d0`, kő `#3e5a70`, jégfény-akcens `#7fd4e8`. Csillagok halványulnak.

**KÉP:** Pre-dawn blue in Medina, a courtyard of horseshoe arches in deep
slate-blue `#3e5a70`, last stars fading in an indigo sky `#0a1626`, scribes
kneeling at low desks copying a single codex into seven identical volumes by
oil-lamp, cold cyan first-light `#7fd4e8` meeting warm lamp glow on the
parchment, reed pens and ink wells, ceremonial stillness.

**VIDEÓ:** Slow glide along the row of seven desks, each quill moving in
near-unison, lamp flames leaning as a draft passes, the horizon behind the
arches turning from indigo to pale cyan. 8s loop.

## X. Turfán — A szerző kánonja (homokba fúlt rom, i. sz. ~800 · szélfútta lap)
Paletta: fakó mályva ég `#3a3448` → csontszín horizont `#c9b8a8`, hamulila föld `#5a4c58`, halvány lila akcens `#d8c8ff`. Sziluett: dűnék + romok. Fátyolos nap.

**KÉP:** A sand-swallowed Manichaean monastery on the Silk Road, bone-white
hazy sky `#c9b8a8` with a veiled sun, mauve-grey dunes half burying broken
walls and a stupa, manuscript pages whirling in the wind like pale birds,
fragments of illuminated script glinting lilac `#d8c8ff`, one intact page
impaled on a dead branch, melancholic emptiness.

**VIDEÓ:** Wind-driven sand streaming over the ruin crest, pages tumbling and
lifting in a slow spiral, the veiled sun pulsing faintly through haze, one page
settling onto the sand in the foreground. 10s wind loop.

## XI. Peking — A Tao raktára (lakk-csarnok, 1406–1445 · nyomódúc és lakk)
Paletta: kék óra ég `#101a30`, lampion-borostyán `#e8a05f`, vörös lakkoszlopok `#7a2e30`, arany akcens `#ffd070`. Csillagok. Sziluett: pagodatetők + város.

**KÉP:** Blue-hour over a Ming imperial hall, star-pricked indigo sky `#101a30`
above glazed roof silhouettes, interior colonnade of red-lacquer pillars

**VIDEÓ:** Slow tracking shot down the pillar colonnade toward a glowing
doorway, lantern light breathing, a craftsman's silhouette crossing with a
